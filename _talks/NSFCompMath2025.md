[The NSF CompMath Meeting 2025](https://sites.google.com/gcloud.utah.edu/nsfcompmath-meeting-2025/home#h.u4s8zbolaggh) took place in Salt Lake City, Utah, May 8–9, 2025.

I presented “Linear Independent Component Analysis in Wasserstein Space” based on joint work with Caroline Moosmueller and Chuxiangbo Wang.

This research was supported in part by the National Science Foundation under grant DMS-2410140.
